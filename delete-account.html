Delete your Aham account

Aham by Mokshabound Studios

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Aham and tap the settings icon on the Today screen.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account and confirm.

Without the app

Email mokshaboundstudio@gmail.com from the Google account email you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Aham account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: Google name, email and account ID
  • Your written affirmation sets and voiced audio
  • Your subscription status and voicing credits

Nothing is kept afterwards, except records Google Play keeps about your purchases, which we do not control.

Before you delete

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions first. Your journal and vision board live only on your phone; to erase them, use Settings › Delete my data on this phone, or uninstall Aham.
